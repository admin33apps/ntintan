4. Sign-in codes and SMS

We send one-time sign-in codes and order, refund and coupon messages by SMS through our provider, Sailup, which receives your phone number and the message text only to deliver it. Sign-in codes are stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form, expire after {{OTP_TTL}} minutes, and are deleted within 24 hours. We never store the code itself in our message log.

Ntintan will never call or message you to ask for a code. If anyone does, it is not us.

5. Marketing

We send marketing messages (offers, newsletters, harvest prices) only if you have opted in, either when you create your account or by subscribing to our newsletter. You can opt out at any time in Account → Profile or by contacting {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}. Order and account messages are not marketing and will still be sent.

6. Who we share it with

  • Paystack, our payment processor, receives your payment amount, reference, and an email address or a phone-based placeholder so it can process Mobile Money and card payments and refunds. Paystack handles your card and Mobile Money details under its own privacy policy.
  • Sailup, our SMS provider, receives your phone number and the message text so it can deliver sign-in codes and notifications.
  • Our delivery staff and courier partners see your name, phone number, delivery address and order contents, so they can deliver.
  • Partner farmers see the products and quantities they need to supply. Your contact details are shared with a farmer only when that farmer delivers to you directly.
  • Hosting, IT and backup providers store and run our systems on our instructions.
  • Professional advisers (lawyers, accountants, auditors), under confidentiality.
  • Authorities such as the police, courts, Ghana Revenue Authority or regulators, when the law requires it or to protect people from harm or fraud.
  • A buyer of our business, if Ntintan is sold or merged, under the same protections.

Everyone who processes data for us must keep it secure and use it only for our instructions. We do not sell or rent personal data.

10. How we protect it

Our safeguards include:

  • encrypted connections (HTTPS) and strict browser security policies;
  • one-time codes stored only in hashed form;
  • staff passwords stored with strong hashing, plus two-step sign-in for staff;
  • role-based access, rate limits and account lockouts;
  • audit logs of sensitive actions, and regular backups.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach is likely to affect you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Commission as soon as reasonably practicable, as Act 843 requires.

11. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), you can:

  • Access your data. Download a copy any time in Account → Profile → Download my data, or ask us.
  • Correct it. Edit your name, email and account type in Account → Profile, or ask us to correct anything else.
  • Delete it. Use Account → Profile → Delete my account. We keep only what the law requires, without your identity (see section 9).
  • Object or withdraw consent. Turn marketing off in your profile, or object to other processing by contacting us.
  • Complain to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana (dataprotection.org.gh) if you are unhappy with how we handle your data.

To exercise a right that isn't available in your account, contact {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}. We may need to confirm your identity, usually by sending a code to the phone number on your account. We will reply promptly and within the time the law allows.

12. Information about other people

If you give us someone else's details, for example a coupon recipient or a person receiving your delivery, please make sure they are happy for you to do so and know about this notice. We use their details only to deliver the coupon or order.

13. Children
